Curatorial Synopsis

Hidden in the heart of the old city of Barcelona is the "Cemetery of Forgotten Books", a labyrinthine library of obscure and abandoned titles in the care of the antiquarian book trade. To this secret precinct, ten-year-old Daniel Sempere is conducted by his bookseller father in the summer of 1945.

Daniel is allowed to choose one book from the shelves and pulls out La Sombra del Viento by Julián Carax. But as Daniel grows, his efforts to discover the author's destiny reveal that someone has been systematically hunting down and burning every last copy of Carax's published works. What begins as a boy’s infatuation turns into an epic descent into a labyrinth of murder, madness, and doomed love across Spain’s darkest decades.
