## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet gravitas of a historic private library combined with the frictionless precision of contemporary digital curation. It serves bibliophiles, collectors, and community book swappers who value physical print culture alongside fluid digital reading.

The design movement is **Refined Literary Editorial meets Tactile Warm Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Evokes archival paper, brass book-ends, oiled oak reading desks, and quiet reading rooms.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial pacing inspired by literary quarterlies—generous margins, deliberate typographic contrast, and restrained decorative flourishes.
- **Physicality:** Tangible book spine visual cues, debossed surface treatments, soft borders reminiscent of deckle-edge paper, and spatial depth tuned to support interactive virtual bookshelf shelves without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic bookbinding materials: deep forest bookcloth, hand-pressed ivory paper, antique gold foil stamping, and weathered vellum.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#173F35` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchor color for dominant actions, focused reading states, navigation framing, and authoritative accents.
- **Secondary (`#C5A46D` - Antique Gold):** Used sparingly for badges of provenance, rare editions, active bookmark states, ratings, and subtle gilded rules.
- **Tertiary (`#A8B9A5` - Muted Sage):** Contextual indicators, secondary tags (e.g., condition reports, community exchange status), and gentle hover states.
- **Neutral (`#292D29` - Deep Charcoal):** Primary reading ink for headlines, body copy, and high-legibility typographic hierarchy.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F5EF` (Warm Ivory) serves as the ambient page color, minimizing eye strain compared to stark white.
- **Surface Elevation / Containers:** `#EEE7DA` (Soft Parchment) for floating cards, drawers, and secondary shelves.
- **Surface Inset / Well:** `#E7DEC9` for depressed metadata plates, book inset slots, and interactive search basins.
- **Borders & Rules:** `#E5DED2` (Soft Beige) provides quiet structural separation without slicing the canvas.
- **Subdued Text / Metadata:** `#77766F` (Warm Gray) ensures metadata such as ISBN, print run, translator notes, and timestamps remain readable yet visually subordinate.

## Typography

The typographic strategy juxtaposes literary editorial tradition with contemporary typographic clarity:
- **Playfair Display** commands all titular, curatorial, and hero moments. Its high stroke contrast and delicate serifs replicate classic typography found on embossed spine titling and title leaves.
- **Manrope** handles all sustained reading, dense inventory data, interface controls, exchange logs, and metadata. Its modern geometric balance preserves legibility even at 10px archival captions.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use italicized variants of `headline-md` and `headline-sm` sparingly for book subtitles, author monikers, and translation notes.
- Use `label-archival` set in all-caps for cataloging IDs, edition numbers, book dimensions, and exchange logistics markers.
- Maintain a generous line-height on `body-lg` (1.66) to replicate traditional printed book proportions for essay reads and long synopses.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure employs a classic editorial grid system with generous breathing room reminiscent of broadsheet margins.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Max-width container capped at `1320px` for catalog views and `760px` for single-reading views.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Interactive shelf rows switch from 3D multi-tier shelves into a single tactile horizontal scroll rail.

### Rhythmic Principles
- Vertical pacing strictly adheres to multiples of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Curated feature sections use `space-2xl` top-and-bottom separation to honor the pacing of physical book chapter breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal paper layering combined with warm ambient light**. Cold grey dropshadows are entirely prohibited.

### Elevation Scales
1. **Flat / Surface Level (Canvas):** `#F8F5EF` with 1px border of `#E5DED2`. No shadow. Used for base views and reading panes.
2. **Elevated Paper (Cards & Volumes):** `#EEE7DA` background with 1px border `#E5DED2` and an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(41, 45, 41, 0.05)`.
3. **Floating Volumes (Interactive Bookshelf Objects):** Used when books are lifted from the shelf or hovered. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 63, 55, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(41, 45, 41, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Overlays & Exchange Drawers:** Backdrop blur (`8px`) over `#F8F5EF` at 85% opacity, paired with an ambient shadow `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(32, 38, 34, 0.18)`.
5. **Debossed Well (Search & Slots):** Inset shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(41, 45, 41, 0.04)` over `#E7DEC9` to mimic recessed library card trays or shelf grooves.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 1 (Soft)**:
- Baseline elements (inputs, tags, standard buttons) use `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radii.
- Surface cards and modal dialogs use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Oversized book jacket display containers use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`).

### Rationale
Bookmaking is an art of rectilinear blocks, pressed board corners, and subtle soft cloth edges. High roundedness or pill shapes dissolve the bookish aesthetic; crisp, soft-cornered geometry reflects trimmed paper and hardcover jackets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Reserve / Exchange):** Solid `#173F35` background, `#F8F5EF` text, 0.25rem border-radius. On hover, background shifts to a deeper forest (`#102D26`) with a subtle `0 2px 8px rgba(23, 63, 55, 0.2)` shadow.
- **Secondary (Inspect / Browse):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#173F35`, `#173F35` text. On hover, background fills with `#A8B9A5` at 20% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Archival:** No borders. Text in `#173F35` with an underline offset of 4px in `#C5A46D`.
- **Gilded Accent Action:** `#C5A46D` background with `#202622` text, used exclusively for primary rare-edition exchanges and verified swaps.

### Chips & Condition Badges
- **Condition Tags (Mint, Good, Reading Copy):** Background in `#EEE7DA`, border 1px solid `#E5DED2`, text in `#292D29` with typography `label-md`.
- **Status Tags (Available for Swap, In Transit):** Muted Sage (`#A8B9A5`) background at 25% opacity, solid `#173F35` text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Recessed background `#E7DEC9` with a subtle 1px border `#E5DED2`.
- Active focus state: 1px border `#173F35` with an outer ring in `#173F35` at 15% opacity (`0 0 0 3px`).
- Placeholder text in `#77766F` set in `Manrope` 14px.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px boxes with `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular 16px for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#77766F` over `#F8F5EF`.
- Checked: Fill `#173F35` with ivory checkmark `#F8F5EF`.

### Cards
- **Book Display Card:** `#EEE7DA` background, 1px solid `#E5DED2`. Includes book cover frame with 1px inset shadow to simulate binding thickness, author line in `#77766F`, title in `Playfair Display`, and exchange badge pinned top-right.
- **Member / Exchange Log Card:** Divided into header, inventory preview, and exchange logistics using 1px horizontal rules of `#E5DED2`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Interactive Bookshelf Rail:** Horizontal shelf surface styled with wood-grain warmth (`#E7DEC9` base, `#DCCFBA` shelf face, with a 2px top highlight `#F8F5EF`).
- **Book Spine Strip:** Compact vertical spine visualization with rotated `Playfair Display` typography, embossed foil effect using `#C5A46D`, and wear-and-tear condition indicators.
- **Ex-Libris Bookplate Stamp:** Decorative community verified badge styled like a woodblock print stamp in `#173F35` with an ornate border.
