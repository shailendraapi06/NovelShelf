Invisible Cities • Italo Calvino

Leaving there and proceeding for three days towards the east, one reaches Diomira, a city with sixty silver domes, bronze statues of all the gods, streets paved with lead, a crystal theater, and a golden cock that crows each morning on a tower.

All these beauties the traveler already knows, having seen them also in other cities. But the special quality of this city for the man who arrives there on a September evening...
